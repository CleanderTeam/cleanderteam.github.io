Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version: your photos and videos never leave your device. Cleander has no accounts and no advertising. It sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google’s Firebase, so that we can see what works and fix what breaks.

This policy describes how the Cleander app for iPhone (“Cleander”, “the app”) handles information. The app is published by Serge German (“we”). Contact: serge.german.dev@gmail.com.

Your photos and videos

Cleander reads your photo library, with your permission, to show your photos and videos and to carry out what you ask: marking, deleting, adding to Favorites and saving edits. All of this happens on your device through Apple’s Photos framework.

  • We do not upload, copy or store your photos or videos on any server.
  • We do not read their content to learn anything about you, and we do not collect their locations, dates or other details.
  • To find exact duplicates, the app compares files on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.
  • Your marks (delete, selected, like) and the app’s settings are kept on your device only.

You can change or withdraw the app’s access to your library at any time in the iPhone’s Settings.

What is collected

The app uses two services of Google Firebase. They receive the following, without your name, email address or any account:

DataExamplesWhy
Usage eventsThe app was opened; an album or the sorting screen was opened and how many items it holds; a photo was marked, skipped, shared or edited; how many photos were deletedTo understand how the app is used and improve it
An app identifierA random identifier that Firebase creates for the installation of the app. It is reset when you delete the appTo count users and sessions and to group crash reports
Crash and diagnostic dataWhat the app was doing when it crashed, the iPhone model, the iOS version and the app versionTo find and fix errors
General locationThe country or region that Google Analytics derives from a shortened IP address. The app does not use location servicesStatistics by country

This information is not tied to your identity, is not used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and is not sold. The app does not show ads and does not ask for permission to track.

Who receives it

Google LLC processes the data above on our behalf through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, and may store it on servers outside your country. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not share data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

When you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, including the app and iOS versions that the app adds to the letter. We use this only to answer you.

How long it is kept

Usage and crash data is kept by Google for the periods set in Firebase and then deleted or made anonymous. Emails are kept for as long as needed to help you. Everything stored on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Your choices and rights

  • You can stop all collection by deleting the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. Because the data is not tied to your identity, we may not be able to find yours; write to us and we will do what we can.

Children

Cleander is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
