Swipe your camera roll clean

Cleander is a photo-cleaning app for iPhone. It shows your photos and videos one at a time, and you decide with a swipe.

Swipe up to send a photo to the trash, right to like it, down to put it in a folder, left to skip it. Nothing is deleted until you confirm it, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
